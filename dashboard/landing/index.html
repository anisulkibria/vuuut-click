A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Getting to know each other starts here.

Will it work with my website?+

Vuuut Click uses a small browser script that works with websites built on any platform that lets you add custom scripts. Add your website, verify its domain, and follow the setup instructions in your workspace.

What can I learn about my audience?+

Explore page views, visitors, sessions, countries, devices, browsers, traffic sources, and campaigns. Track custom events and create goals for important actions on your website.

How does visitor privacy work?+

The tracker uses random identifiers and supports consent controls. It respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control, and raw IP addresses are not stored with events. Your collection settings and consent requirements still need to fit your audience and applicable rules.

Can I run it on my own infrastructure?+

Yes. Vuuut Click can run on your own infrastructure with the same analytics API and dashboard. Your self-hosted installation does not need a connection to a hosted analytics service.
